Advice to younger me: ignore most, if not all, of the thoughts you have. (they come from conditioning)

If you need to think: write, speak, or draw instead. (fresh thoughts flow here)

And when your mind yanks you into a thought, note it, and come back to the raw sensations of your body.

This is moment to moment embodiment.
