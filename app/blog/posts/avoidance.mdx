*"What am I feeling in my body that I am avoiding?"*

Bruce Tift inspired this question. And I really like it. When you ask, attention auto-magically moves to the sensations you're feeling *and* avoiding.

Which is cool because the question assumes you're already feeling something and you're already avoiding whatever that is.

So when you ask:
- follow where the question takes you
- feel the raw sensations as they arise
- attend to those sensations with kindness

It doesn't make sense to avoid anything within you. Why reject yourself? What positive purpose is there to doing that. Do you become more "yourself" when you reject yourself? Does it bring you closer to yourself or away?

The answer is pretty straightforward once you look into it.
