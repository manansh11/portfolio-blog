*"What am I feeling in my body that I am avoiding?"*

Bruce Tift inspired this question. And I really like it. 

Because it helps attention move to the sensations you're feeling *and* avoiding

This is cool because the question assumes you're already feeling something and you're already avoiding whatever that is.

So when you ask:
- follow where the question takes you
- feel the raw sensations as they arise
- attend to those sensations with kindness
