---
title: "Enjoyment creates value"
date: "2025-08-11"
description: "How enjoyment creates value"
---

Take what is unenjoyable and go through the process of making it enjoyable.

The process of making an unenjoyable thing an enjoyable one is creating value.

Imagine a situation:
- It's unenjoyable to experience right now
- You come up with a creative way to make it more enjoyable
- Which other people may or may not resonate with

How you do this is up to you. What you find unenjoyable may not be true for other people and the solutions you come up with may not resonate with others. But that's ok!

Because you end up with 2 outcomes, either:
1. You've created value for yourself
2. You've created value for yourself *and* others

Both of which are awesome and amazing.
